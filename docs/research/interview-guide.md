# Interview guide: five short conversations

Purpose: find out whether product managers and analysts who have to explain a metric gap would use something like decision-pack, and what they do today. This is a plan. No interviews have happened, and `findings.md` stays empty until they do.

## Who to talk to

Five people, 20 minutes each. Aim for a mix: two product managers, two product or data analysts, one growth or marketing analyst. People who have explained a metric movement to a non-technical audience in the last three months.

## Before you start

- Say what this is: research for a side project, not a sales call. You are not selling anything.
- Ask permission to take notes. Do not record unless they agree.
- Do not show the tool until the last five minutes. You want their current behaviour first.
- Write down what they did, not what they say they would do.

## Script

**1. Context (2 min)**
- What is your role, and what metrics do you own or report on?

**2. A recent case (8 min)** Ask for one specific recent time a number moved and they had to explain it.
- Walk me through the last time you had to explain why a metric was worse for one group than another. What happened?
- What did you start with? Which tools?
- How did you decide which segment or factor to look at first?
- How did you split the gap between causes, if you did? How sure were you of that split?
- Did anyone challenge it? What did they say?
- How long from the question to an answer you would defend?

**3. Output (4 min)**
- What did you send to the people who had to decide? A message, a slide, a document?
- How did you size what to do about it? Where did the numbers come from?
- What did you leave out because you could not support it?

**4. Pain (3 min)**
- What was the slowest or most annoying step?
- What would you stop doing by hand if you could?
- Has an analysis ever been wrong in a way you only noticed later? What happened?

**5. Reaction (3 min)** Show the sample pack from `cases/funnel-lab/pack/`.
- What do you understand this to be saying, in your words?
- What would you not trust, and why?
- What is missing?
- Would you use this on your next case? What would stop you?

## Questions to avoid

"Would you use a tool that...?" and "Do you think this would be useful?" invite polite yes answers. Ask about the last time, not the next time.

## After each interview

Within an hour, fill one row in `findings.md`: role, the case, the first thing they did, how they split the gap, what they sent, the pain, the reaction. Quote only what you wrote down at the time.

## What would change the plan

- If nobody splits a gap between drivers at all, the bridge is solving a problem people do not have; lead with the segment scan instead.
- If people already trust a specific tool for the split, find out why before building more.
- If the deck is never used, cut it and put the effort into the memo.
